Discuss custom software ↗

When off-the-shelf tools force your process into their shape, we build the system that fits. We start from the decisions your team makes every day, then design the data and the interface around them.

Think approvals that disappear into chat, reports assembled by hand, or work that depends on one person remembering every detail. A shared workspace gives those processes a clear owner, a visible status, and a record your team can return to.

The goal: fewer workarounds, clearer decisions, and software that belongs to your business.

Included

  • API and service design
  • Control dashboards and management portals
  • Database and domain modelling
  • Role-based access and audit trails

Put your company knowledge to work. We design AI-assisted workflows that can find relevant information, organise an incoming request, or prepare a useful draft for a person to review.

We work through the data, access rules, evaluation criteria, and handoff points before deciding how much autonomy an agent should have. Quality, operating cost, and human oversight are part of the design.

The goal: less repetitive handling, with your team in control of important decisions.

Included

  • Agent and workflow design
  • LLM integration and prompt architecture
  • Evaluation sets and regression checks
  • Guardrails, logging, and cost controls

Corporate sites, landing pages, and customer portals with the accessibility and performance budgets a public product needs. Built to be edited later, not rebuilt.

A website should explain the business, answer the questions a customer brings, and make the next action obvious. We connect the content, interface, and underlying systems so the experience works beyond its first impression.

The goal: a clearer customer experience and a platform you can keep improving.

Included

  • Marketing sites and landing pages
  • Customer and partner portals
  • Design systems and component libraries
  • Performance and accessibility budgets

When a sale, enquiry, or update has to be entered twice, the gap is between the systems. We connect the tools your business already uses and define what should happen when something changes.

That can mean sending an enquiry into a CRM, syncing records between services, or turning an event into a follow-up task. Retries, logs, and failure handling make the integration usable when real operations get messy.

The goal: information reaches the right place without another copy-and-paste routine.

Included

  • Third-party and internal API integrations
  • Webhooks and event-driven sync
  • WhatsApp CRM and messaging pipelines
  • Scheduled jobs and error monitoring
02 Process

Clear steps.
Something real at every stage.

You should be able to see what is being built, understand the tradeoffs, and know what happens next. Each phase turns the conversation into something you can review.

  1. Phase 01

    Discovery and blueprint

    We map the systems you run today, find where work gets stuck, and agree on the architecture before any code is written.

    You get

    • System map
    • Architecture decisions
    • Delivery plan
  2. Phase 02

    Build in sprints

    Two-week sprints, each ending with a working build on staging. You review the real thing and redirect while redirecting is still cheap.

    You get

    • Staging build
    • Sprint demo
    • Updated backlog
  3. Phase 03

    Deploy and hand over

    We ship to your infrastructure, set up monitoring and backups, and train your team on what we built. Then we step back.

    You get

    • Production deployment
    • Monitoring and alerts
    • Backup policy
    • Handover session

Where we are headed

Our mission is to become an AI Native Company.

Advancing technology through research, development, and real-world implementation.

We want AI to become part of how Arstech learns, works, and builds products. That means developing our own ways of working alongside the systems we create for others.

Our ambition is to contribute to technology as it develops: investigate useful ideas, turn them into working systems, and learn from what happens when people use them.

Bring us a problem worth exploring ↗

How we intend to get there

Research

Understand before we build.

Explore new methods, test assumptions, and evaluate where a technology can make a practical difference. Start with a problem and evidence that the approach fits.

Develop

Turn an idea into a working system.

Connect models, data, software, and people. Build prototypes that reveal the limits early, then refine the parts that hold up under real requirements.

Implement

Learn from real use.

Bring the technology into actual workflows. Define ownership, review points, and ways to measure quality so an implementation can improve after launch.

AI helps with the work. People remain accountable for the decisions.
